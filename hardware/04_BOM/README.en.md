# Robot bills of materials

Both upstream bills of materials are based on RP1.3.4. Project part numbers have been converted to ROBOSKIN; manufacturer part numbers are preserved.

| Configuration | Bill of materials |
| --- | --- |
| 5DOF_MotionHead - project baseline | [BOM](ROBOSKIN_BOM_5DOF_MotionHead.xlsx) |
| 7DOF_MotionHead - upstream alternative | [BOM](ROBOSKIN_BOM_7DOF_MotionHead.xlsx) |

Both workbooks contain a complete materials sheet and a packaging-materials sheet. The complete sheet lists assemblies, structural parts, standard parts, boards, harnesses and purchased components, including part number, name, quantity, revision and material.

Select the BOM for the appropriate configuration and find documents by part number. [Mechanics](../01_Mechanic/README.en.md), [boards](../02_PCB/README.en.md), [harnesses](../03_Harness/README.en.md).

Skin additions are documented separately in the [skin component list](../../electronics/system_bom.csv) and [88-sensing-site example](../../electronics/example_88_site_bom.csv). The example is a generic test configuration, not proof of fit to a robot. Record location, quantity, mass, power supply and cable routing according to the [integration plan](../skin_integration.en.md) before adding items to the robot bill of materials.
