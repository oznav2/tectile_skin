# Project scope and source attribution

The skin circuit specification, generic mechanical templates and sensor software are original project implementations. The [hardware](hardware/README.en.md) directory now contains an adapted RoboParty RP1 hardware package, imported at the project owner's request with ROBOSKIN identifiers, English working filenames and Hebrew document-text translation. Imported designs are not claimed as original ROBOSKIN inventions.

The repository has independent Git history and is neither a submodule nor a copy of the source history. This independence does not remove licensing and attribution obligations for derivative files. The [hardware and documentation licence mapping](hardware/LICENSES.en.md), [original CERN licence](hardware/LICENSE), and [pinned source/file mapping](hardware/provenance/README.en.md) are included. Supplier and third-party materials retain their terms. Attribution does not imply partnership, sponsorship or endorsement by the hardware source.

Published ideas such as distributed tactile acquisition, send-on-delta reporting, skin calibration and wrench mapping are attributed in the plan and literature review. Inclusion is not a claim of novelty. Manufacturer part numbers, standards and electrical ratings retain their identity rather than being renamed as project branding.

Research papers are linked rather than bundled. The evidence register distinguishes full-text, partial, abstract-only and citation-only reading. This snapshot claims no certification, measured sensor result, commercial breakthrough or completed manufacturing qualification of the adapted hardware package.
